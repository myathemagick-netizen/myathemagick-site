# BOOK SIX: FELLOW TRAVELERS

*Independent arrivals at the same geometry*

A pataphysical model is strengthened, not threatened, by other people arriving at its shape from completely different roads. This closing book gathers two such arrivals: a Soviet astrophysicist measuring rotation as a physical medium in a laboratory, an architect-philosopher finding the same icosahedron in the geometry of maximum structural efficiency, and a touring performer who found a riftline node under Central Park without ever having read a word of theory about it.

## Kozyrev and Fuller --- The Grid Confirmed From Outside

Nikolai Kozyrev's torsion field research --- the experimental
investigation of time as a physical medium with measurable properties
that respond to rotation --- provided the physical vocabulary for what
the system needed to describe. Kozyrev's finding that left-handed and
right-handed rotation produce measurably different effects on local
physical systems mapped directly onto the Levogyre/Dextrogyre
distinction. The chronon field as a torsion phenomenon. The phase-lock
as a forced dextrogyre rotation of what should be a levogyre flow.
Kozyrev did not know about Atlantis or Earth Pillars. He was measuring
the physical substrate of everything the system describes.

Buckminster Fuller's synergetics --- the geometry of maximum
efficiency, the principle that nature solves problems through minimum
energy and maximum flow, the geodesic dome as an architectural
expression of Regular system geometry --- provided the structural
foundation for understanding why the icosahedron was the Earth's
natural chronon distribution geometry. Fuller understood that the
universe builds with the same geometric principles at every scale. The
system takes that insight further into consciousness, mythology, and
civilizational theory. Fuller stayed largely in the physical and
architectural domain. The system takes the same principles into
territory he didn't map.


## S.K. Thoth --- The Riftline Finds Its Practitioner

S.K. Thoth --- performance artist, composer, and creator of the Festad
solopera --- was encountered in Central Park in 2007 and proved to be
one of the most directly catalytic influences on the practical dimension
of the system. Thoth performs what he calls prayformance: a fusion of
original violin composition, spinning dance, percussion from bells and
chimes worn on the body, and singing in a constructed language of his
own invention --- the language of the Festad, a mythological world he
has been developing since childhood. His practice is not entertainment
with spiritual overtones. It is a sustained ritual technology that
operates simultaneously at the linguistic, musical, kinesthetic, and
spatial levels.

The specific location of Thoth's primary New York performance --- the
Angel Tunnel beneath Bethesda Terrace in Central Park --- is not
incidental. The tunnel is one of the most acoustically resonant spaces
in the city, a vaulted stone structure whose proportions create standing
wave patterns that concentrate sound energy at specific nodes. The
tunnel sits within what the system identifies as a significant riftline
corridor running through the eastern park, near Cleopatra's Needle --- a
3,500-year-old Aswan granite obelisk whose anomalous weathering rate in
New York, documented by Egyptologists, is consistent with the kind of
chronon stress that accumulates at an active riftline node operating
within a phase-locked geometry. Thoth did not choose this location
through theoretical analysis. He was called there. The riftline found
its practitioner.

Walking back with Thoth to the subway after performances, in
conversation about language, music, the nature of the constructed world
of the Festad, the idea of an infinite language that could express what
ordinary language cannot --- these conversations were a direct catalyst
for the infinite language project that has been an active thread of the
Work ever since. The encounter with a living practitioner of riftline
maintenance technology --- someone doing it without the theoretical
framework but doing it correctly and persistently and at considerable
personal cost, having instruments broken, having the park services
repeatedly attempt to shut down the practice --- demonstrated that the
system was describing something real that people could feel and were
compelled to do even without a language for why.

The Spirit Catcher that opens and closes each prayformance traces its
lineage to Darrell DeVore (1939--2005), a San Francisco experimental
musician and instrument builder who developed the original bipyramid
wind wand form in the city's free-music scene. Thoth encountered
DeVore's instrument tradition before relocating to New York in 1999 and
developed the Spirit Catcher from it --- adding the foldable bipyramid
geometry and integrating it as the formal ritual boundary marker of the
prayformance. The instrument's full lineage runs from DeVore back
through the bullroarer tradition to the Paleolithic, a continuous
20,000-year thread of riftline opening technology that the SF music
scene and then the NYC prayformance tradition kept alive. Thoth calls
his global touring practice a Walkabout, reaching intuitively for the
same conceptual framework that the Australian First Nations songline
tradition uses. This is not appropriation but recognition: the same
functional dynamic independently perceived and named with the closest
available word. Thoth and Lila'Angelique now live in Ubud, Bali --- one
of the most significant riftline nodes on the planet --- continuing the
practice at a location where it is less actively suppressed than at the
Angel Tunnel. Their work can be found at skthoth.com and is worth
seeking out as one of the clearest living examples of what levogyric
performance practice looks like when operating at full expression.
