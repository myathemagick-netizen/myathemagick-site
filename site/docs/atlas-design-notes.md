# Atlas design notes and proposed content illustrations

Implemented locally: book-group headings now use 36px at desktop with a subtle lavender glow, while chapter titles use 21px and descriptions 16px. Chapter navigation uses a thin muted plum scrollbar with a transparent track. Header backdrops reuse approved assets: Atlas artwork for the introduction and cosmological chapters; RiVViT for Book Two and geography; Audio Shear Zone for practice; Levogyre for fellow travelers. Serpent and architectural line motifs distinguish Books Three and Five. These are illustrative backdrops, not evidence for the model.

Suggested additions to preview before inserting into prose:

| Chapter | Most useful addition | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction / Closing Note | A small visual key separating geometric structure, historical references, and speculative interpretation | Help readers understand how to read the map |
| Book One | Labelled counter-rotation diagram, showing two rotations and their shared boundary; icosahedron showing vertices, faces, and edges | Make the central geometry understandable without interrupting paragraphs |
| Book Two | Labelled RiVViT view and a simplified corridor map with numbered edges | Help readers follow the geography; captions should distinguish the proposed model from measured geography |
| Geography subpages | One map per group highlighting only its named corridors, plus links to the interactive globe | Allow a reader to locate each place without scanning the whole globe |
| Book Three | Serpent / songline motif comparison with attributed, licensed references | Show the symbolic connections discussed in the text without suggesting the traditions are interchangeable |
| Book Four | A practical flow diagram and a labelled Audio Shear Zone screenshot | Give readers a visual path from an idea to a practice |
| Book Five | A timeline of the sites and acoustic technologies discussed, with source notes | Help readers compare periods; keep historical dates and speculative connections distinct |
| Book Six | Levogyre screenshot plus a diagram of the geometry discussed; licensed portraits only if useful | Connect the named influences to their actual work |

Prefer one useful figure per major idea, with a caption and readable labels. Keep generated atmospheric illustrations clearly labelled as illustrations. Reuse header assets; avoid large decorative images between every few paragraphs. New raster illustrations should be previewed and approved before being added.
