# World Atlas

Ten lands, each with its own ground, sky, sound and music, and the places that stand in them.

## The lands

- [Overgrown Plains](/game/atlas/biomes/plains): Open grassland gone to seed: the old farm country.
- [Autumn Fields](/game/atlas/biomes/autumn): Old open country gone to tan grass, goldenrod and asters, the trees turned orange, red and gold.
- [Woodland](/game/atlas/biomes/woodland): Thick, old woods, dark under the leaves.
- [Wetland](/game/atlas/biomes/wetland): Low, wet ground: lakes, reeds and marsh, dead trees standing in the water.
- [Rocky Highlands](/game/atlas/biomes/highlands): Bare rock and steep slopes where little grows.
- [Mountains](/game/atlas/biomes/mountains): Ranges of bare rock, pines below, snow on the tops that never melts.
- [Rustbelt](/game/atlas/biomes/rustbelt): Mile on mile of dead industry: the ground built over, levelled, poisoned.
- [Hollow Wood](/game/atlas/biomes/hollow): An old, dark forest in a twilight of its own, with a moon too large.
- [Sporelands](/game/atlas/biomes/bloom): Where the network breaks the surface.
- [Glass Desert](/game/atlas/biomes/desert): A vast, sun-baked plain of sand and pale mineral crust, fused to glass in places.
