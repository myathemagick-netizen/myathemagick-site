# Testing and feedback

The most useful things to try, in the order the game teaches them: scavenge a town, forage and eat mushrooms safely, craft and write a research book, build a shelter, then go looking. The newest content is the **Glass Desert** (hot by day, cold by night): carry a canteen, go in the morning or the evening, and look for the service stations, solar farms and the broken highway.

## What to send back

- The **build number** (in the window title).
- What you were doing, where (the compass bar names the land), and a **screenshot** (F12) if it is a visual problem.
- Whether it is a **bug** (something broke), a **balance** note (too easy, too hard, too slow) or a **feel** note (a sound, a look).
- Sound problems: name the sound if you can (for example "a mycelium footstep buzzes").

## Known limits of the preview

No combat, no villagers or allies, no weather, no strains or affinity, no Root Lines (see [Future Systems](/game/future)). Some numbers are untuned, especially in the newest desert content. World saves from much older builds may show wrong blocks.

**Where to send it:** not recorded. The author must say whether feedback goes to a form, an email, a Discord or Ko-fi messages before this page is published.
