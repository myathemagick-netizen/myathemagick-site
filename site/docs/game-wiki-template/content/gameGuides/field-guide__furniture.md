# Furniture

Everything you can place or find standing in the world. **X** = use, **hold X** = pick up,
**hold RT** = dismantle. Each piece's loot is rolled once (seeded by where it stands);
dismantling an unsearched container also gives its contents. Found food rolls its own tier
(fridges skew towards Rot). Pictures are the in-game models.
