# Buildings and structures

Towns are laid out on a road grid and filled with buildings stamped from authored templates: houses, apartments, offices, data centres, shops
(general store, furniture, electronics, grocery, hardware, clothes, pharmacy, laundromat), a post office, farmhouses and barns, factory halls and
warehouses in the Rustbelt, and the landmarks (museum, library, hospital). Every building rolls two independent conditions when it is made: how
ruined it is (standing, damaged, heavily damaged, ruin) and how far the fungus has grown through it. The same building generated twice can
feel completely different.

Street and yard props (mailboxes, bus stops, traffic lights, benches, billboards), parks and playgrounds, and wild trees, rocks and giant mushrooms
are placed by the same code. A generated documentation page shows every template from outside and floor by floor.

*Source:* `source_docs/buildings.md` is the author's generated building documentation (with its pictures in the bundled `game_render` images).
The design intent (kit of pieces, two variation axes) is in `source_docs/gdd.md` section 2.
