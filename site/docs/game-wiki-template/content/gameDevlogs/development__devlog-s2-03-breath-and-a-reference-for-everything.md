# Breath, and a reference for everything

With health and hunger on screen, being under water needed its own meter. A
**breath** bar appears when your head goes under. It lasts about twenty
seconds, then you start drowning, and it refills quickly in air. Adding a
third bar pushed hunger off the bottom of the screen until the bar stack was
set to grow upward.

There's now an item & block reference (source document), with an icon,
what it drops or does, where to find it and what it breaks into for every
block and item. It's generated by a Godot script from the same data the game
uses, so it can't drift out of date. Mushroom edibility sits behind a
spoiler, since in the game you learn it by eating.
