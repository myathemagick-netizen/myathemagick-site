# The Autumn Fields, drive-ins, and squirrels

- **A sixth land, the Autumn Fields** (id `autumn`; from a photo of an old
  field going back to the wild): among the plains, from its own noise field.
  Long tan grass gone to seed (thick: about six cells in ten have something
  growing), goldenrod and asters, scattered trees in their fall colours
  (orange, red, gold leaves: three new leaf blocks), wild pumpkins (a block)
  in the grass, and old parking lots of cracked asphalt and gravel going under
  the weeds. Golden grass tint, rust mushrooms. Its Journal page, picture and
  symbol; towns lean to groceries and furniture shops.
- **Acorns** from the tall grass (the squirrels hid them) and autumn leaves;
  roasted at a campfire (four for a handful). **Pumpkin soup** at the hearth
  (pumpkin and greens: filling, and it keeps you going). **Jack-o'-lanterns**:
  carve one at the workbench (pumpkin and a torch); set it down and it glows.
- **Roadside in the Autumn Fields:** **drive-in theatres** at the end of their
  own gravel drive (the screen on log posts, rows of cars still facing it,
  speaker posts, the projection booth with a vending machine and counter, a
  billboard by the road; on the compass, in the Journal's places), and
  **pumpkin stands** on the verges (a farm stand, pumpkin piles, hay). Both in
  buildings.md.
- **Squirrels, the first animals** (`world/critters.gd`): one lives by every
  other autumn tree (and now and then a woodland one). By day they hop about
  in short bursts, stop to forage and sit up to look round; come within 6 m
  and one dashes to its tree, runs up the trunk and hides in the leaves until
  you're 14 m off, then comes down head first. They stand on the real ground
  (found from the blocks, not the plan), and they're gone at night. A tan
  squirrel with a rust back and the network's teal in its tail.
  (`critters_test`)
