# Journal pages as lists; the pot's recipes; hand-drawn icons; room for trophies

- **Mushrooms, Brews and Places** pages work like Lore: a list down the left,
  the one chosen on the right.
  - **Mushrooms**: every wild mushroom you've picked or eaten; safe, sick or
    untried; its description; what it does in a brew (with Mycology 2).
  - **Brews**: every brew you've made or drunk; its ingredients and its
    effects as icons, how long it lasts; brews you've made but not drunk
    are listed by what went in ("Untasted: Tan Button Mushroom + Inkcap").
  - **Places**: each kind found, with a picture of the building (from the
    buildings doc) and its lore; each kind has its own symbol.
- **The brewing pot remembers what you've made**: "Brews you've made" under
  the Brew button. Pick one to fill the pot (greyed if you're short of a
  mushroom). Experimenting is how a mix gets on the list; you can still put
  mushrooms in by hand. The pot's controls show in the corner hints.
- **Hand-drawn icons** for the eleven trophies, and symbols for the places.
- **Room for big things**: placing something larger than a block (the
  mounted tyrannosaur, three by seven and four high) checks its whole
  footprint is clear and on level ground ("Not enough room here").
