# Sounds for the mountains

- `goat_bleat` (3 takes, trimmed and levelled): a goat bleats from where it
  is as it bounds off.
- `amb_mountains` (2 seamless 22 s loops): cold wind over bare rock, a far-off
  raven now and then. In the mountains it plays day and night, in place of
  `amb_day` and `amb_night`.
- ElevenLabs sound effects; prompts and generation IDs are in
  asset_src/audio_sources.json.
