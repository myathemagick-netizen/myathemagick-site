# A sound pass

Nine new sounds (ElevenLabs, 24 takes; levelled with tidy_sfx.py), each
wired to what it's for:

| Sound | Plays when |
|---|---|
| `bag_open` | you open a bag with your pack |
| `sort` | you sort slots |
| `shelve` | a crate goes onto a storage shelf |
| `pick_plant` | you break a plant (was the soft-block sound) |
| `spores_puff` | you break fungal growth: tufts, mats, blooms, hyphal rust |
| `brush_grass` | you walk through tall grass, reeds, ferns or weeds, under the footstep |
| `globe_spin` | you spin a globe |
| `map_open` | you turn to the Journal's Map page |
| `amb_landmark` | you're inside a museum, a library or a hospital: a hush, in place of the street's wind |

Still on the wishlist: a few one-offs (poster tear, scrub, bones, glass
case, swing creak), weather.
