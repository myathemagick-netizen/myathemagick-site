# The manor's entrance hall, and the wallpapers

From the author's screenshots: the entrance hall was a patchwork of the
neighbouring rooms' papers, with rails set into solid walls.

- **Halls wear one paper**: any wall a hall touches takes the hall's paper
  (funeral stripe or blood damask, one per manor). A room keeps its own
  paper on its other walls; the wall it shares with a hall shows the
  hall's on both faces (walls are one block thick).
- **Gallery rail** only where the gallery is open to the hall; where a
  room stands there the wall is left whole.
- **The floor over the front door is gone**: that bay is open to the first
  floor like the rest of the entrance hall (it could not be reached).
- **Rails up both sides of the grand stair**, on closed strings.
- **Paintings** on the entrance hall's side walls, on both levels.
- **Wallpapers**: faded fleur, dead chrysanthemum and autumn acanthus have
  small motifs now (four to a block, staggered). All ten have wear: damp
  stains, the pattern rubbed thin in patches, and the same block-to-block
  variation the plain wallpaper has.
- Not done: separate ruined or mycelial versions of the papers.
