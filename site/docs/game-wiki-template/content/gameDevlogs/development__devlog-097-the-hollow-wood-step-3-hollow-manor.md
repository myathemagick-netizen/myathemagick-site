# The Hollow Wood, step 3: Hollow Manor

The house on the estate's site (`hollow_manor`, theme `manor_gothic`), its
stoop at the head of the drive.

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Outside | 29 by 19 m, three storeys of `gothic_stone` (new: dark, plum-grey) under a steep `roof_slate_dark` roof; a square tower at each front corner rising six blocks past the eaves to a pointed roof |
| Inside | two great rooms to a floor: hall and dining room; library and bedrooms; the master's rooms and a nursery with an easel. Switchback stairs at the back of the west rooms |
| Attic | a flight from the top floor's east room. The open attic has crates and old furniture; both towers open off it |
| The walled-off room | the west end of the attic, and the west tower over it, behind a plank wall with no door: a bed, a candelabra, crates, a case of rare books. You break in |
| Light | a `chandelier` in each room (four in ten still burn), `candelabra` (lit). The rest is dark |
| Furniture | `grand_piano` ("Play"), `grandfather_clock`, fireplaces, patterned sofas, fine armchairs |
| Paintings | four found only here, never paintable at an easel: The Lady of the House, Hollow Manor by Moonlight, The Rider, The Family. They can be taken down and hung at home |

- Not built: the basement panic vault (templates have no storeys below
  ground yet), a grand stair, bigger windows.
- `estate_test` covers the house too.
