# Furniture and doors

Fourteen more voxel props: wood and glass doors, beds, sofas, armchairs,
tables, chairs, bookshelves with missing books, kitchen counters and sinks,
fridges, half-looted store shelves, crates and an old tube TV (a nod to the
"communication" research line).

![Furniture](/images/encyclopedia/game/screenshots__2026-09-30__13_furniture_props.jpg)

*Development gameplay capture*

A furnishing pass puts the right items in each room (kitchen, living room,
bedroom, shop floor) against walls, keeping doorways and stairs clear. Ruin
removes some furniture and doors; overgrown rooms sprout mushrooms on the
floor and shelf fungus on the walls. Rotating a building rotates its
furniture with it, which a test row of four rotations checks.
