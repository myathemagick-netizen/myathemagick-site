# Topic bags

- **Ten bags that each hold one kind** (the colours of §70): Woodsman's
  (wood & fibre; Carpentry), Mason's (stone & earth; Masonry), Prospector's
  (metal & ore; Mining), Tinker's (electronics; Electronics), Tailor's
  (cloth; Tailoring), Forager's (mushrooms; Mycology), Cook's (food;
  Cooking), Medic's (medicine; First Aid), Alchemist's (brews and bottles;
  Alchemy), Scholar's Satchel (books and paper; Communication).
- **Made at the sewing table with Tailoring and their own skill**: Sprout (9
  slots) at 2 and 2, Mycelial (18) at 3 and 3, Fruiting (27) at 4 and 4.
- **They fill themselves:** what you pick up (or make) of their kind goes
  into one you carry (on your back, or anywhere in your pack) before your
  pack. **Stash** (a button on the pack) moves everything of their kinds out
  of the backpack into them; the hotbar stays as it is. Things you take out
  of a bag yourself stay out.
- They're bags like the others (open with your pack / B, set down as
  storage, on a storage shelf); a shelf of topic bags by a station is a
  sorted store.
- Models: each dyed its kind's colour, something of its trade sticking out
  (planks, a brick and chisel, a pick, a coil of wire and a board, yarn and a
  needle, mushrooms, a loaf and a ladle, a white cross and a bandage, a
  potion, scrolls); icons to match.
