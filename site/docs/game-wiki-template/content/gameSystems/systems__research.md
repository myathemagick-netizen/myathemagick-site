# Research


## Research books and knowledge
<a id="research-books-and-knowledge"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Every action teaches its topic; a book of that topic in the study slot records it; five levels per topic unlock recipes and stations; a Knowledge tab maps what you know; topics open from other topics.

**Limits:** Teaching allies with books is a design idea only.

**See also:** [Library and communication](/game/systems/research#library-and-communication)

**Development:** [7](/game/development/devlog-007-research-knowledge-from-doing-written-into-books), [17](/game/development/devlog-017-tailoring-cooking-and-five-level-books), [18](/game/development/devlog-018-metal-electronics-glass-and-alchemy-topics-that-open-others), [23](/game/development/devlog-023-communication-the-library-two-books-at-once-paintings), [34](/game/development/devlog-034-what-you-ve-learned-stays-learned)


## Library and communication
<a id="library-and-communication"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Writing at a desk teaches Communication; a library of 18 research books, a second study slot, an easel for paintings, faster copying.
