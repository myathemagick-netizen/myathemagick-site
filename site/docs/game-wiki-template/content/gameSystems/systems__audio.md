# Audio


## Sound and music
<a id="sound-and-music"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Per-land ambience and music (the author's Suno tracks), generated sound effects for footsteps, stations, creatures and weather-less air, volume sliders.

**Limits:** Effects made with ElevenLabs carry a faint 200 Hz hum, which a cleaning pass reduces but may not fully remove.
