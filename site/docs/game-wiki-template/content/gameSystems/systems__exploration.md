## Cartography
<a id="cartography"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Maps drawn by walking, a cartography table, better maps, pins, a minimap, globes, wall maps and atlases.

## Compass and places
<a id="compass-and-places"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

A compass bar with places you have found (towns, farms, quarries, stations, solar farms, highways...), distances and land names.

**See also:** [Cartography](/game/systems/exploration#cartography)

**Development:** [36](/game/development/devlog-036-a-compass-bar)


## Journal and milestones
<a id="journal-and-milestones"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

A Journal with mushrooms, brews, places, biomes, lore, milestones with hidden rewards, and found-out tallies; the diary form of the Mile Stone.
