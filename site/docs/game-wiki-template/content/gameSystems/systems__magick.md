# Magick


## Brewing and effects
<a id="brewing-and-effects"></a>

**Status:** Experimental (new, untuned or partial)

A brewing pot, a bucket of water and one to three mushrooms make a brew; each mushroom leans toward an effect (strength, luck, night sight, regeneration...); effects are learned by drinking; poison agarics can make bad brews; an alembic distils stronger tinctures.

**Limits:** This is the 'mushroom brews' branch of the GDD's magick only. 'Seeing the unseen' is a status effect with no perception layer yet; no addiction or tolerance.
