# Engineering


## Power, water and machines
<a id="power-water-and-machines"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Hand crank, water wheel, battery banks, electric lamps, solar panels, a lithium bank, steam (boiler and engine), biodiesel (oil press, fuel still, diesel generator), mycodiesel (culture bench, bioreactor), tesla tower and stormcap; pipes, pumps and tanks with wrench and multimeter views.

**Limits:** No power grid connectivity simulation; ranges are proximity rules.

**See also:** [Research books and knowledge](/game/systems/research#research-books-and-knowledge)

## Mining and metallurgy
<a id="mining-and-metallurgy"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Ores by depth, caves and darkness, pickaxe and drill, quarries and old mines; metallurgy turns scrap and ore into iron and steel; goggles and an ore detector.
