# Creatures


## Squirrels, goats, rats, crows, owls
<a id="squirrels-goats-rats-crows-owls"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Ambient animals with simple behaviours: squirrels in autumn and woodland trees by day, mountain goats, rust rats at night, crows by day and owls by night in the Hollow Wood.

**Limits:** They flee or watch; none attack and none can be hunted yet.

**See also:** [Combat and weapons](/game/future/combat-and-weapons)

**Development:** [64](/game/development/devlog-064-the-autumn-fields-drive-ins-and-squirrels), [79](/game/development/devlog-079-the-mountains-step-3-of-3-mountain-goats), [84](/game/development/devlog-084-the-rustbelt-step-3-of-3-hazards-life-and-a-way-to-heal-it), [103](/game/development/devlog-103-the-hollow-wood-step-4-crows-and-owls)


## Dune Hopper
<a id="dune-hopper"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

A nocturnal desert jerboa: hops in arcs, bolts at 8 m and goes down its burrow.

**See also:** [Glass Desert](/game/systems/world#glass-desert)

**Development:** [131](/game/development/devlog-131-the-glass-desert-step-5-the-dune-hopper)

## Glassback Beetle
<a id="glassback-beetle"></a>

**Status:** Experimental (new, untuned or partial)

A day beetle on the glass fields with a glassy back: crawls, sits in the sun, runs and hides at 5 m.

**Limits:** No loot or interaction yet.

**See also:** [Glass Desert](/game/systems/world#glass-desert)

**Development:** [141](/game/development/devlog-141-the-glassback-beetle)
