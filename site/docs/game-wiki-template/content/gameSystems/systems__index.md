# Game Systems

What works in the current development build. Each system is marked **implemented** or **experimental** (new, untuned or partial).


## [Audio](/game/systems/audio)

- Sound and music

## [Building](/game/systems/building)

- Masonry, kiln and rot-proof building
- Building and base furniture

## [Crafting](/game/systems/crafting)

- Crafting
- Stations and ladders
- Tools
- Cooking
- Tailoring and clothing
- Cultivation

## [Creatures](/game/systems/creatures)

- Squirrels, goats, rats, crows, owls
- Dune Hopper
- Glassback Beetle *(experimental)*

## [Engineering](/game/systems/engineering)

- Power, water and machines
- Mining and metallurgy

## [Exploration](/game/systems/exploration)

- Cartography
- Compass and places
- Journal and milestones

## [Interface](/game/systems/interface)

- Controller support and hints
- Hide the interface (F10)
- Graphics settings *(experimental)*
- Saves and title screen

## [Magick](/game/systems/magick)

- Brewing and effects *(experimental)*

## [Research](/game/systems/research)

- Research books and knowledge
- Library and communication
- Field notes and hidden recipes

## [Survival](/game/systems/survival)

- Hunger, health and food safety
- Cold and warmth
- Heat
- Canteen and bottled water *(experimental)*
- Climate hazards of the lands
- Swimming and breath

## [World](/game/systems/world)

- Voxel world and terrain
- Ten biomes (lands)
- Towns and buildings
- Furniture, containers and loot
- Landmarks: museum, library, hospital
- Country roads
- Roadside places
- Mountains
- Rustbelt and the works
- Hollow Wood and Hollow Manor
- Sporelands
- Glass Desert
- Glass Desert landmarks *(experimental)*
- Day, night and sky
- Mycelium spread and ground cover
- Weathering

Things that are only planned or imagined are under [Future Systems](/game/future); they are not part of the game you can play.
