# Crafting


## Crafting
<a id="crafting"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Hand crafts on the Character screen and station crafts at workbench, sewing table, campfire and more; recipes appear after you have held an ingredient; crafting takes a moment and tools wear out.

**See also:** [Research books and knowledge](/game/systems/research#research-books-and-knowledge), [Tools](/game/systems/crafting#tools)

**Development:** [3](/game/development/devlog-003-room-to-breathe-dropping-storage-stacks-and-gear), [8](/game/development/devlog-008-crafting-takes-a-moment-tools-and-wearing-them-out), [12](/game/development/devlog-012-the-kiln-wood-tar-treated-planks-stairs-and-slabs)

## Stations and ladders
<a id="stations-and-ladders"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Station ladders unlocked by research: sewing table to loom to textile machine; campfire to hearth to iron stove to smokehouse and kitchen range; masonry bench to kiln to furnace to blast furnace; electronics bench; glassblower's bench; alembic.

**Limits:** Carpentry and Mycology ladders are not finished.

**See also:** [Research books and knowledge](/game/systems/research#research-books-and-knowledge)

**Development:** [17](/game/development/devlog-017-tailoring-cooking-and-five-level-books), [18](/game/development/devlog-018-metal-electronics-glass-and-alchemy-topics-that-open-others)


## Tools
<a id="tools"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Hatchet, pry bar, spade, knife, pickaxe, rock drill; uses left shown on a bar; worn-out tools break into scrap, never vanish.

**See also:** [Crafting](/game/systems/crafting#crafting)

**Development:** [8](/game/development/devlog-008-crafting-takes-a-moment-tools-and-wearing-them-out), [30](/game/development/devlog-030-mining-part-a-what-s-under-your-feet)


## Cooking
<a id="cooking"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Campfire to kitchen range; better meals leave lasting effects like brews.

**See also:** [Hunger, health and food safety](/game/systems/survival#hunger-health-and-food-safety)

**Development:** [17](/game/development/devlog-017-tailoring-cooking-and-five-level-books)


## Tailoring and clothing
<a id="tailoring-and-clothing"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Cloth, clothes and gear in tiers, with warm and light kits for cold and heat.

**Limits:** Armour-style protection against creatures does not exist (no combat).

**See also:** [Cold and warmth](/game/systems/survival#cold-and-warmth), [Heat](/game/systems/survival#heat)

**Development:** [17](/game/development/devlog-017-tailoring-cooking-and-five-level-books), [121](/game/development/devlog-121-the-warm-rung-gear-for-the-peaks), [122](/game/development/devlog-122-the-heat)


## Cultivation
<a id="cultivation"></a>

**Status:** Implemented

Planters (potatoes, beans, greens), compost, mushroom logs and mycorrhizal compost, growing in game time with visible stages.

**Limits:** Farming beyond mushroom cultivation is lightly developed.

**See also:** [Brewing and effects](/game/systems/magick#brewing-and-effects)

**Development:** [24](/game/development/devlog-024-growing-food-and-mushrooms-crafts-between-two-fields-the-knowledge-map)
