# Mycelium affinity and strain wheel

> **Status: CONCEPTUAL.** A design idea, not a commitment, and not playable.

Strains sit on a colour wheel; consuming a strain shifts you toward its traits; wheel distance decides hostility, trade, compost outcomes and transport efficiency. Choosing a strain also chooses which opposite strain is hostile, which is meant to be the replayability engine.

**Where it stands:** Only placeholder strain colours exist on mushrooms and cultures; no affinity meter, no wheel, no hostile territory.

**Source:** GDD section 3; the full taxonomy waits on a reading pass.
