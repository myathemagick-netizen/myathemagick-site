# Future Systems

> Nothing on these pages is playable. **Planned** means decided and wanted; **conceptual** means a design idea from the game design document that may change or never be built.

[Design pillars](/game/future/design-pillars) · [Roadmap](/game/future/roadmap)

## Planned

- [Weather](/game/future/weather): Rain, storms, dust storms and heat waves: hooks exist in the climate code and sound/visual effects are filed, but no wea
- [Combat and weapons](/game/future/combat-and-weapons): Melee, bows, scavenged firearms, spore weapons; creatures that fight back.
- [Cordyceps-infected mobs](/game/future/cordyceps-infected-mobs): Fungus-animated 'zombies' with ambiguous purposes.
- [Enchantment and ritual magick](/game/future/enchantment-and-ritual-magick): Item enchantment and ritual or luck magick, beside the brews that exist.
- [Wildlife expansion](/game/future/wildlife-expansion): Fox, hare, moth, ironhart, horseman and others.
- [Quests](/game/future/quests): Quest chains beyond milestones.
- [More weather and dust storms](/game/future/more-weather-and-dust-storms): Dust storms, rain blooms and a general weather system.
- [Felling whole trees](/game/future/felling-whole-trees): Chainsaw tiers and whole-tree felling.

## Conceptual

- [Mycelium affinity and strain wheel](/game/future/mycelium-affinity-and-strain-wheel): Strains sit on a colour wheel; consuming a strain shifts you toward its traits; wheel distance decides hostility, trade,
- [Root Lines (fungal transport)](/game/future/root-lines-fungal-transport): Underground fungal tunnels as a transport network, grown or found, efficient by strain alignment, with a hybrid mycelium
- [Repopulation and villagers](/game/future/repopulation-and-villagers): Cure or convert infected people into allies who may be new mycelium-human life, with their own agency; train allies with
- [Automation](/game/future/automation): Industrial, ally-trained and hybrid automation paths.
- [Mycelium 3D printer](/game/future/mycelium-3d-printer): A late-game printer that grows fungal-hybrid items from cultivated mycelium.
- [Composting and scrapping by strain](/game/future/composting-and-scrapping-by-strain): Time-gated composting that turns non-metal junk into mushrooms and parts by the strain it meets; metal reclaimed by heat
- [Seeing the unseen](/game/future/seeing-the-unseen): A perception layer (network, infection, Root Lines) from mushroom brews.
- [Factions](/game/future/factions): Strain-aligned and neutral groups of allies and rivals.
