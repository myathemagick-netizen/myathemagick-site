# Design pillars

*From the game design document (section 1). These describe intent, not the current build.*

- **Mycelium as a planetary process, not an enemy faction.** It behaves like weather: it spreads, decays, blooms and reclaims on its own clock.
- **Environmental storytelling over explicit lore.** The cause of the catastrophe is deliberately never fixed; players build their own theory from what they find.
- **Multiple valid paths to every system.** Magick, automation, transport and repopulation each offer two or three legitimate approaches.
- **Rediscovery through reverse-engineering, not a tech tree.** Research is active: form an expectation, test it, observe. Rare things are studied, not simply unlocked.
- **One consistent relationship rule** (the strain colour wheel's distance) for hostility, trade, composting and transport. *Not built yet.*
- **Real-world grounding.** Fungal mechanics draw on real mycology (mycoremediation, mycofiltration, plastic-eating and radiotrophic fungi).

**What exists today** of these: the ambient, spreading mycelium (Sporelands, ground cover), environmental storytelling in generated ruins and journals, research by doing, and mushroom foraging, brewing and cultivation. See [Game Systems](/game/systems).
