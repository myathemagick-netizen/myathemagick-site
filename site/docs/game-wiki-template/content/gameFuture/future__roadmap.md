# Roadmap: crawl, walk, run

*The author's intended order of work from the design document (section 16). These are intentions, not promises or dates.*

**Crawl (foundation).** Voxel terrain and a destructible world; the basic survival loop; a visible spreading mycelium; mushroom foraging and brewing; things never simply disappear when they wear out. **Largely built.**

**Walk (distinctive).** Ruin scavenging and blacksmithing; a simple affinity system; cordyceps-infected creatures; enchantment magick; basic composting; research books; Root Lines as mysterious tunnels. **Scavenging, blacksmithing (metallurgy) and research books are built; the rest is not.**

**Run (deep and expensive).** Glassworking to alchemy; repopulation with villagers who have agency; ally and industrial automation; the 'see the unseen' perception layer; ritual magick; the full colour-wheel affinity system; the mycelium 3D printer; functional Root Lines. **Glassworking and alchemy are built in a first form; the rest is not.**

Biome work followed its own order: the land map, roads, collapse stories, a landmark per land. Ten lands exist now; collapse stories per town are only partly built.
