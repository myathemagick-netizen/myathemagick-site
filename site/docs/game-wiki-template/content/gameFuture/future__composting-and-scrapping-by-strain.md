# Composting and scrapping by strain

> **Status: CONCEPTUAL.** A design idea, not a commitment, and not playable.

Time-gated composting that turns non-metal junk into mushrooms and parts by the strain it meets; metal reclaimed by heat.

**Where it stands:** Worn tools do break into scrap and stay useful, but composting is not built.

**Source:** GDD section 6; the 'never simply disappears' rule is already followed by tools.
