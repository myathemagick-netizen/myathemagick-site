# Hollow Wood

*An old, dark forest in a twilight of its own, with a moon too large.*

An old, dark forest in a twilight of its own. The trees are huge and red-leaved, the ground is years of fallen leaves, and a mist lies along it that never quite lifts. The moon is too large here. People kept out of it long before the collapse.

## What you can find

- Ancient trees, some hollow: room to shelter inside
- Wine-red leaves, deep leaf litter, ferns and moss
- Rust agarics, shelf fungus and inkcaps, thick under the trees
- Mist along the ground; a moon too large
- Crows by day (they don't let you near); owls by night, watching
- An estate deep inside, if the wood is wide enough: Hollow Manor

## Good to know

- Bitterroot, a bitter medicinal root, grows under its old trees.
- Graveyards stand out in the wood, by the road where there is one.
