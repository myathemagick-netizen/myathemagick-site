# Glass Desert

*A vast, sun-baked plain of sand and pale mineral crust, fused to glass in places.*

A vast, sun-baked plain of sand and pale mineral crust, long low dunes under a washed-out sky. Hot by day and cold by night; nothing grows but dry scrub. Whatever fused the ground to glass is long gone, and the shade is far apart. Go in the morning or the evening, carry water, and don't be caught in the open at midday.

## What you can find

- Sand and bleached mineral crust, long dunes
- A heat that follows the sun: dangerous at midday, mild in the morning and evening
- Dry scrub, thorny bushes and the odd cactus
- Sand truffles under the dry washes
- A service station by the road at the edge: the canopy is shade
- No towns: only what was left of the road

## Good to know

- Shard dunes cut bare feet unless the footwear protects; blackglass flats and fulgurite towers stand in the deep desert; crystal spires rise in the glass fields (clear on shards, black on blackglass).
- The Dune Hopper comes out at night; the Glassback Beetle by day.
- A very large sun, drifting sand, glints, and heat shimmer.
- Landmarks: service stations at the margin, solar farms and a broken highway out in the deep desert.
- Its cause is deliberately unexplained.
