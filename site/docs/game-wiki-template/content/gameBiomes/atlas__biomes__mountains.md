# Mountains

*Ranges of bare rock, pines below, snow on the tops that never melts.*

Ranges of bare rock rising far above the lowlands, pines on their lower slopes, snow on the tops that never melts. Caves run up inside them, and their rock is seamed with ore. Nobody settled up here; the towns kept to the valleys.

## What you can find

- Ore all through the rock: coal, iron, copper, tin; gold near the tops
- Caves inside the mountains
- Pines on the lower slopes; heather and white alpine flowers
- Snow above the snowline
- Old mines along the mountain roads
- Fire lookouts and cairns on the summits; trails of stone steps down
- Stone huts near the treeline
- Mountain goats on the open slopes, by day

## Good to know

- The cold gets harder with height and at night; warm gear in five tiers helps.
- Snow blows past on the peaks.
