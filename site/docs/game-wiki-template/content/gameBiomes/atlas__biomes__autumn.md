# Autumn Fields

*Old open country gone to tan grass, goldenrod and asters, the trees turned orange, red and gold.*

Old open country gone to long tan grass, goldenrod and asters, the trees in it turned orange, red and gold. Under the grass, the old parking lots and foundations are going back to the field.

## What you can find

- Pumpkins in the long grass
- Acorns: under the trees and in the grass
- Old parking lots breaking up under the weeds
- Farmsteads, lone houses and shops by the roads
- Rust buttons and rust agarics

## Good to know

- Drive-in cinemas stand at the end of their own gravel drives; pumpkin stands sit on the verges.
- Squirrels live in the trees by day.
