# Overgrown Plains

*Open grassland gone to seed: the old farm country.*

Open grassland gone to seed: the old farm country. Easy walking and long views. A few trees, many of them dead.

## What you can find

- Farmsteads by the roads: their fields still grow wild potatoes, beans and greens
- Lone houses and shops
- Lines of cars left nose to tail on the roads

## Good to know

- The baseline land: the usual story here is evacuation (doors open, cars in lines on the roads out).
