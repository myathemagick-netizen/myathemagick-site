# Rustbelt

*Mile on mile of dead industry: the ground built over, levelled, poisoned.*

Mile on mile of dead industry: the ground built over, levelled, poisoned. Black soil full of rust, slag in heaps, scrap rusting into it, and pools of foul water that burn the skin. Hardy fungi feed on the metal in the ground; the sky over it never looks right.

## What you can find

- Scrap heaps: scrap metal, wire, now and then a pipe
- Pools of foul water: don't wade in them
- Shelf fungi and rust mushrooms feeding on the metal
- Old factory floors and railway ballast under the weeds
- The works: factory halls, warehouses, ironworks and tank farms, catwalks between them over the streets
- Rail yards full of rusted boxcars
- Ironcaps: a metal-eating mushroom. Cultured, it cleans a foul pool
- Rust rats, out at night: in the dark, just their red eyes
- Rust that cuts: wrap your hands before you break it (tetanus)

## Good to know

- Green creeps against the rust in places; what is left standing tells what happened.
