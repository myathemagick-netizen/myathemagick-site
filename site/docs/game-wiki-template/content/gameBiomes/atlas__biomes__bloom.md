# Sporelands

*Where the network breaks the surface.*

Where the network breaks the surface. The ground is felted with mycelium and tinted teal; giant mushrooms stand over it, and the network spreads twice as fast as anywhere else. Trees rot where it's strongest.

## What you can find

- Giant mushrooms, teal and tan
- Spore blooms, teal buttons and agarics
- Mycelium soil (it seeds itself)
- Overgrown cars, mats heaving up through the roads

## Good to know

- Giant mushrooms stand 16 to 24 blocks tall; the teal ones glow at night.
- It has its own ambience and music.
