# Woodland

*Thick, old woods, dark under the leaves.*

Thick, old woods, dark under the leaves. Logs for the taking, and fallen trunks across the verges.

## What you can find

- Trees for logs, everywhere
- Tall agarics in tan and rust; button mushrooms
- Farmsteads and lone houses by the roads
- Overgrown cars

## Good to know

- Squirrels live in about one tree in six.
