# Wetland

*Low, wet ground: lakes, reeds and marsh, dead trees standing in the water.*

Low, wet ground: lakes, reeds and marsh, dead trees standing in the water. The roads cross it on plank boardwalks.

## What you can find

- Inkcaps and puffballs in the damp
- Water, reeds and clay
- Lone houses and shops by the roads

## Good to know

- Lily pads float on its shallow lakes; clay for the kiln is dug from its beds.
