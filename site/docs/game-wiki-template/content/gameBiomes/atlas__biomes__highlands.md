# Rocky Highlands

*Bare rock and steep slopes where little grows.*

Bare rock and steep slopes where little grows. Stone outcrops, rockfalls on the roads, cairns someone stacked. The network spreads slowest here.

## What you can find

- Quarries and old mines: the place for ore
- Puffballs and rust buttons
- Stone and gravel

## Good to know

- The usual collapse story is a sealed-in town: boarded doors and stocked cellars.
