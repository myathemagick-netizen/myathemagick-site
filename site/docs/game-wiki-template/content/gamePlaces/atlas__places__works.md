# The works

What's left of the industry. Factory halls with their lathes and crane hooks, warehouses of empty shelves, ironworks under their stacks, tank farms, rail yards of rusted boxcars, catwalks across the streets between the upper floors. A fortune in metal; foul water in every hollow.
